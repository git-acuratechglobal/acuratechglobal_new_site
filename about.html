Our Journey

The AcuraTech Story

From a small startup to a global technology partner — here's how we got here.

2014
Founded
AcuraTech Global was founded in India & Global with a vision to make enterprise-grade technology accessible to businesses of all sizes.
2016
First 100 Clients
Reached a landmark milestone of 100 happy clients across the US, Canada, and the UK, cementing our reputation for quality delivery.
2018
Global Expansion
Opened development centers in India and Europe, extending our talent pool and enabling round-the-clock delivery for global clients.
2020
500+ Projects Delivered
Surpassed 500 successfully delivered projects spanning web, mobile, cloud, and cybersecurity domains.
2022
ISO 9001 Certified

2024
AI Division Launched
Launched a dedicated Artificial Intelligence & ML division to help clients harness the power of generative AI and intelligent automation.

To be the world's most trusted technology partner — a company where businesses of every size can access cutting-edge digital solutions that drive real growth, operational excellence, and competitive advantage. We envision a future where every organization is empowered by intelligent, human-centric technology.

🎯

Our Mission

To deliver innovative, scalable, and reliable technology solutions that transform how businesses operate and compete. We combine deep technical expertise with genuine client partnership, ensuring every project creates measurable value. Our commitment is to quality, transparency, and long-term success for every client we serve.
